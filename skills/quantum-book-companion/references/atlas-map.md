## Part 1 — Orientation: The Territory and Its Scale

- [The Quantum Landscape](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-technology-landscape/) — A map of the whole field: the foundations of quantum theory, the technologies built on them, and how mature each one is.
- [Where Quantum Matters](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-scales/) — Quantum behaviour shows up when dimensionless comparisons tip one way: a system's action is not much larger than the reduced Planck constant, its thermal energy is below the spacing of its energy levels, or its matter wavelength or coherence length reaches the size of the structure it moves through; when they tip the other way, classical descriptions become good approximations.

## Part 2 — What Nature Does: The Quantum Phenomena

- [Quantisation of Energy](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/energy-quantization/) — Bound systems can hold only certain discrete amounts of energy, and light exchanges energy with matter in indivisible packets.
- [Wave-Particle Duality and Interference](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/wave-particle-duality/) — Every quantum object travels like a wave and is detected like a particle, as the two-slit experiment shows one click at a time.
- [Superposition](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/superposition/) — A quantum system can exist in a weighted combination of distinct states at once, and the weights can interfere.
- [Quantum Measurement](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-measurement/) — Measuring a quantum system yields one definite outcome at random, with probabilities set by the state, and leaves the system changed.
- [The Uncertainty Principle](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/uncertainty-principle/) — Certain pairs of properties, such as position and momentum, cannot both be sharply defined for the same state, whatever the measuring device.
- [Quantum Tunnelling](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-tunnelling/) — A quantum particle can pass through a barrier that it does not have enough energy to climb over.
- [Spin and Angular Momentum](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/spin/) — Particles carry a built-in angular momentum called spin, which for an electron gives exactly two outcomes along any measured direction.
- [Identical Particles: Bosons and Fermions](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/identical-particles/) — Quantum particles of the same kind are truly indistinguishable, which sorts all of them into bosons that crowd together and fermions that exclude one another.
- [Entanglement](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-entanglement/) — Two or more quantum systems can share a joint state in which the whole is fully specified although no part has a definite state of its own.
- [Bell's Theorem and Nonlocality](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/bell-nonlocality/) — No theory in which particles carry pre-set answers and influences travel no faster than light can reproduce the correlations that quantum mechanics predicts and experiments observe.

## Part 3 — How It Is Written: The Language of Quantum Theory

- [The Quantum State](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-state/) — The quantum state is a list of complex numbers called amplitudes that holds everything that can be predicted about a system.
- [The Qubit](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/qubit/) — A qubit is the simplest quantum system, with two basis states, whose possible pure states cover the surface of a sphere.
- [Observables and Operators](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/observables/) — Each measurable quantity is represented by an operator whose special values are the possible results and whose order of application matters.
- [The Schrödinger Equation and Unitary Evolution](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/schrodinger-equation/) — Between measurements a quantum state changes smoothly and reversibly, at a rate set by the system's energy operator, the Hamiltonian.
- [Symmetry and Conservation Laws](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/symmetry-and-conservation/) — Whenever a quantum system looks the same after a transformation, a corresponding quantity is conserved and its states sort into families labelled by quantum numbers.
- [Driving and Controlling Quantum Systems](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-control/) — A two-level system driven at its resonance frequency swings smoothly between its two states, and shaped sequences of such pulses steer quantum systems toward chosen states despite noise.
- [The Quantum Harmonic Oscillator](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-harmonic-oscillator/) — Anything that vibrates about an equilibrium has evenly spaced energy levels and cannot be completely at rest.
- [Composite Systems and the Exponential State Space](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/composite-quantum-systems/) — The state of several quantum systems together lives in a space whose size multiplies with each added system, so most joint states cannot be split into separate parts.
- [Mixed States and the Density Matrix](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/density-matrix/) — A density matrix describes a quantum system when our knowledge is incomplete or when the system is entangled with something we cannot see.
- [Generalised Measurement and Tomography](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/generalized-measurement/) — A general quantum measurement is any information-gathering interaction, described by its outcome probabilities and the state changes they cause, of which the textbook projective measurement is a special case.
- [Decoherence](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/decoherence/) — Decoherence is the loss of observable quantum interference that occurs when a system becomes entangled with an environment that cannot be tracked.
- [Open Systems and Noise Channels](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-channels-and-noise/) — An open quantum system exchanges energy or information with surroundings that are not observed, and a quantum channel is the map that says what this noise does to any input state.
- [Interpretations of Quantum Mechanics](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/interpretations-of-quantum-mechanics/) — The interpretations are rival accounts of what the quantum formalism says about reality, all of which agree on every prediction tested so far.

## Part 4 — What It Is Made Of: Quantum Light and Matter

- [Atomic Structure](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/atomic-structure/) — Electrons bound to a nucleus occupy discrete orbitals whose energies, shapes and fine splittings give each element its fingerprint.
- [Light-Matter Interaction](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/light-matter-interaction/) — Atoms and light exchange energy one quantum at a time through absorption, spontaneous emission and stimulated emission, and a cavity can make this exchange coherent and reversible.
- [Photons and Quantum States of Light](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-states-of-light/) — Light is a quantum system whose states range from laser-like coherent beams to single photons and squeezed light with noise below the usual limit.
- [Molecules and the Chemical Bond](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/chemical-bonding/) — Atoms bind into molecules because shared electrons occupy lower-energy orbitals spread over several nuclei.
- [Energy Bands in Solids](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/band-theory/) — In a crystal the electron levels of many atoms merge into allowed bands separated by forbidden gaps, which decides whether the solid conducts, and confining electrons to nanometre structures makes their levels discrete again.
- [Many-Body Quantum Physics and Emergence](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-many-body-physics/) — When many quantum particles interact, new collective behaviours and particle-like excitations emerge that no single particle has.
- [Superconductivity and the Josephson Effect](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/superconductivity/) — Below a critical temperature some materials carry current with zero resistance and expel magnetic fields because their electrons pair up and move as one quantum state.
- [Bose-Einstein Condensation and Superfluidity](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/bose-einstein-condensation/) — Cooled far enough, a gas of bosons collapses into a single quantum state and behaves as one giant matter wave that flows without friction.
- [Topological Phases of Matter](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/topological-phases-of-matter/) — Some phases of matter are defined by a global, knot-like property of their quantum state, which protects certain behaviours against specified local perturbations for as long as an energy gap and any required symmetries hold.
- [Quantum Fields and the Vacuum](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-field-theory/) — Quantum field theory treats every kind of particle as an excitation of a field that fills space, uniting quantum mechanics with special relativity.

## Part 5 — What It Allows in Principle: Information, Precision and Computation

- [No-Cloning and Quantum Teleportation](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-teleportation/) — An unknown quantum state cannot be copied, but it can be moved: teleportation transfers it to a distant system using shared entanglement and two classical bits, destroying the original.
- [Quantum Information Theory](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-information-theory/) — Quantum information theory measures how much information quantum systems can hold, share and transmit, and treats entanglement as a quantifiable resource.
- [Quantum Thermodynamics](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-thermodynamics/) — Quantum thermodynamics studies heat, work and entropy when coherence, correlations and the fluctuations of small systems matter, including the energy cost of information.
- [Quantum Limits of Measurement](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-metrology/) — Quantum mechanics sets the ultimate precision of any measurement, and entangled or squeezed probes can beat the limit that independent particles obey.
- [Quantum Gates, Circuits and Models of Computation](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-circuits/) — A quantum circuit is a sequence of elementary operations called gates applied to qubits and ending in measurement; it is the standard way to write a quantum computation, other universal models can run the same computations with modest overhead, and restricted models such as boson sampling cannot.
- [What Quantum Computers Can and Cannot Do](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-computational-complexity/) — Quantum complexity theory classifies which problems a quantum computer could solve efficiently, by how much it could beat classical methods, and which problems stay hard even for it.
- [Period Finding and Shor's Algorithm](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/shors-algorithm/) — Shor's algorithm finds the prime factors of a large number efficiently on a quantum computer by turning factoring into the search for a hidden period.
- [Amplitude Amplification and Grover's Algorithm](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/grovers-algorithm/) — Grover's algorithm finds a marked item among N possibilities in about the square root of N steps by repeatedly rotating amplitude toward it, a quadratic speedup over checking them one by one.
- [Simulating Quantum Systems on a Quantum Computer](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/hamiltonian-simulation/) — A quantum computer can imitate the time evolution of another quantum system step by step, using resources that grow only modestly with the size of that system.
- [Classical Simulation and Its Limits](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/classical-simulation-of-quantum-systems/) — Classical computers can imitate quantum systems efficiently whenever entanglement is limited or the dynamics has special structure, and this boundary keeps moving.

## Part 6 — Already at Work: Established Devices and Enabling Technology

- [Lasers and Photonic Devices](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/lasers-and-photonic-devices/) — Lasers, light-emitting and light-detecting devices, and the sources and detectors that emit or count light one photon at a time all work by controlled quantum transitions.
- [Laser Cooling and Trapping](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/laser-cooling-and-trapping/) — Laser light and electromagnetic fields can slow atoms and ions to within a millionth of a degree of absolute zero and hold them in place.
- [Quantum Electronics, Magnetic Resonance and Standards](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-electronics/) — Transistors, tunnel devices, Josephson circuits, magnetic-resonance scanners and the electrical standards of the SI are established technologies that exploit quantum effects in large ensembles of electrons, electron pairs or spins.
- [Enabling Technologies](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-enabling-technologies/) — Enabling technologies are the refrigerators, lasers, electronics, detectors and fabrication processes without which no quantum device can be built or run.

## Part 7 — The Instruments: Quantum Sensing

- [Atomic Clocks](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/atomic-clocks/) — An atomic clock keeps time by locking an oscillator to the fixed frequency of a transition between two atomic states.
- [Atom Interferometry](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/atom-interferometry/) — An atom interferometer splits and recombines matter waves with laser pulses to measure gravity, acceleration and rotation.
- [Quantum Magnetometry and Local Sensing](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-magnetometry/) — Quantum magnetometers and local sensors detect weak magnetic fields, electric fields and temperature through the shifts these cause in superconducting loops, atomic vapours, Rydberg atoms or single spins in diamond.
- [Quantum-Enhanced Interferometry and Imaging](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-imaging/) — Quantum-enhanced imaging and interferometry use squeezed or entangled light to see or measure with less noise than ordinary light of the same power allows.

## Part 8 — The Links: Communication and Cryptography

- [The Quantum Threat to Cryptography](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-threat-to-cryptography/) — A large fault-tolerant quantum computer would break the public-key cryptography that secures most digital communication today.
- [Post-Quantum Cryptography](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/post-quantum-cryptography/) — Post-quantum cryptography is ordinary software cryptography built on mathematical problems that quantum computers are not known to solve efficiently.
- [Quantum Key Distribution](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-key-distribution/) — Quantum key distribution lets two parties create a shared secret key whose secrecy is guaranteed by the laws of measurement and not by computational difficulty.
- [Quantum Random Number Generators](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-random-number-generators/) — Quantum random number generators produce random bits from inherently unpredictable quantum measurements rather than from algorithms or noisy classical processes.
- [Quantum Networks, Repeaters and the Quantum Internet](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-networks/) — A quantum network links distant quantum devices by distributing entanglement, and because quantum signals cannot be amplified, long links need repeaters that store entanglement in memories and join short segments by swapping.

## Part 9 — How It Survives: Error Correction and Fault Tolerance

- [Quantum Error Correction and Codes](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-error-correction/) — Quantum error correction protects a qubit of information by spreading it over many physical qubits and repeatedly checking for errors without ever looking at the information itself.
- [The Surface Code and Fault Tolerance](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/fault-tolerant-quantum-computation/) — Fault tolerance lets a quantum computer compute reliably for as long as needed although every component is faulty, provided physical errors stay below a threshold; the surface code, which needs only checks between neighbours on a grid, is the code most hardware plans rely on.
- [Logical Qubits in the Laboratory](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/logical-qubits/) — A logical qubit is an error-corrected qubit built from many physical ones, and experiments now show its error rate falling as the code grows.

## Part 10 — The Machines: Building Quantum Computers

- [Anatomy of a Quantum Computer](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-computer-architecture/) — A quantum computer is a layered system in which qubits, control electronics, error correction and classical software must all work together, and most users reach it through the cloud.
- [Superconducting Qubits](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/superconducting-qubits/) — Superconducting qubits are electrical circuits cooled near absolute zero that behave as artificial atoms, controlled and read out with microwave pulses.
- [Trapped-Ion Qubits](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/trapped-ion-qubits/) — Trapped-ion qubits are single charged atoms held in vacuum by electric fields, with quantum information stored in their internal states and gates driven by lasers or microwaves.
- [Neutral-Atom Qubits](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/neutral-atom-qubits/) — Neutral-atom qubits are individual atoms held by focused laser beams and made to interact by exciting them to large Rydberg states.
- [Photonic Quantum Computing](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/photonic-quantum-computing/) — Photonic quantum computing encodes information in particles or modes of light and computes by interference, measurement and feed-forward.
- [Solid-State Spin Qubits](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/solid-state-spin-qubits/) — Solid-state spin qubits store information in the spin of single electrons, holes or nuclei confined in semiconductor devices or crystal defects.
- [Quantum Annealing](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-annealing/) — Quantum annealing searches for low-energy solutions of an optimisation problem by slowly reshaping an energy landscape in a network of coupled qubits.
- [Analog Quantum Simulators](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/analog-quantum-simulation/) — An analog quantum simulator is a controllable quantum system built to behave like another quantum system that is too hard to calculate.
- [Qubit Platforms Compared](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/qubit-platforms/) — Qubit platforms are the competing physical systems, from superconducting circuits to single atoms and photons, in which quantum computers are being built, compared here on common axes together with topological qubits, which are still a proposal.

## Part 11 — The Work: Using Quantum Computers

- [Computing on Noisy Machines: Variational Methods and Error Mitigation](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/noisy-intermediate-scale-quantum-computing/) — Noisy intermediate-scale quantum computing tries to get value from today's uncorrected processors by using short circuits, classical optimisation loops and statistical correction of errors.
- [Quantum Computing for Chemistry and Materials](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-chemistry-and-materials/) — Quantum simulation of chemistry and materials uses a quantum computer to calculate the energies and properties of molecules and materials that defeat classical approximation.
- [Optimisation and Machine Learning](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-optimization-and-machine-learning/) — Quantum approaches to optimisation and machine learning use annealing, parameterised circuits or quantum algorithms inside a search for good solutions or a learning task, and so far have the thinnest evidence of advantage among the proposed uses.
- [Quantum Advantage and Its Cost](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-advantage/) — Quantum advantage is a quantum device doing a task beyond the practical reach of any classical computer; useful advantage means doing a valuable task better once every cost of the full workflow is counted.

## Part 12 — The Verdict: Judging the Field

- [Benchmarks, Verification and Standards](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-benchmarks/) — Quantum benchmarks are the standard measurements used to say how good a quantum device is, and verification and standards decide whether the evidence supports a stated capability and can be reproduced.
- [How to Read a Quantum Claim](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/reading-quantum-claims/) — Reading a quantum claim means checking what was actually shown, at what scale, against what alternative, and by whom it was verified.
- [Roadmaps, Programmes and Policy](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-roadmaps-and-policy/) — Roadmaps and programmes are the published plans of companies and governments for quantum technology, and policy is the set of public choices (funding, security, export control, standards, access) that shapes how the field develops.
- [Where Effort Pays](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-strategy/) — A quantum strategy is an organisation's reasoned choice of what to act on now, what to prepare for, and what merely to watch, across the quantum technologies.
- [Open Problems of the Technology](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/open-problems-in-quantum-technology/) — The open problems of the technology are the unsolved scientific and engineering questions that decide which quantum technologies will matter, and when.

## Part 13 — Coda: Where Quantum Theory Is Still Open

- [Quantum Frontiers: Gravity and the Limits of Quantum Theory](https://ouviaj.com/the-atlas-of-quantum-science-and-technology/companion-snapshots/quantum-frontiers/) — The frontiers are where quantum theory is still untested or incomplete: its meeting with gravity and curved spacetime, and its validity for ever larger, heavier and warmer objects.
